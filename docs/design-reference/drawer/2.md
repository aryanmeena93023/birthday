## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a cinematic, tactile archival mystery: an intersection between an antique mahogany academic sanctum at NLUJA Assam and an intimate, candlelit midnight birthday consecration. It evokes solemn scholastic reverence, intellectual secrecy, and deeply tender ceremonial warmth. The user experience does not mimic a standard software dashboard; it behaves as an interactive physical drawer, a velvet-lined casket of sealed letters, aged leather folios, and brass-fitted lockboxes uncovered under the beam of single-point ambient candlelight.

The design movement combines **Tactile / Skeuomorphic Realism** with **Atmospheric Minimalist Restraint**. The interface rejects flat cards and generic modern containerization in favor of organic layering: aged paper stock with deckled borders, brass inlays, velvet recessed troughs, and radial chiaroscuro illumination.

Key emotional pillars:
- **Solemn Archival Reverence:** Precision cataloging, wax-sealed provenance, and clandestine institutional intimacy.
- **Midnight Ceremony:** The luminous, flickering warmth of celebratory intimacy breaking through midnight shadows.
- **Tactile Weight:** Every element feels heavy, crafted, polished by human touch, and aged with dignity.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes tension between historical prose and institutional preservation:
1. **Ceremonial & Narrative Voice (EB Garamond):** Used for intimate epistles, literary titles, and ritual invocations. Large displays leverage soft optical spacing, italicized inflection, and classic book proportions.
2. **Archival & Ledger Control (Space Mono):** Used for precise catalogue numbers, timestamps, encryption states, access level indicators, and latitude/longitude coordinates. Rendered with wide letter-spacing to mimic cold, typewritten ledger plates and etched brass placards.

Rules for implementation:
- Italicized `EB Garamond` must be reserved for emotional reflections, personal notes, and literary quotes.
- `Space Mono` must always be rendered in full uppercase for label roles (`label-caps`, `label-xs`), using subtle letter-spacing (`0.15em` to `0.25em`) to balance the tactile density of dark-mode backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional spatial composition representing an antique mahogany writing desk viewed from above under focused illumination.

### Desktop & Widescreen
- Operates on a 12-column asymmetric focal grid. Rather than filling columns wall-to-wall, content sits within a generous sanctuary margin (`3.5rem`), prioritizing breathing space that evokes a secluded room at midnight.
- The primary artifact (letter, cipher roll, drawer tray) occupies the inner 8 columns (max-width `960px`), flanked by 2-column archival metadata gutters on both sides.

### Tablet & Compact Screens
- Collapses to a 6-column fluid structure. Outer margins contract to `2rem`. Archival metadata shifts from side margins into an accordion footer drawer or anchored parchment docket.

### Mobile Screens
- Single-column focal flow with `1.25rem` outer canvas margins. Elements stack vertically, preserving an envelope-like unfolding interaction where tactile cues, stamps, and ribbons lead downward into the narrative core.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use synthetic drop shadows or flat elevation borders. Depth is realized through natural physical strata and candlelight falloff:

1. **Vignette Foundation (Tier 0 - Abyss):** The canvas edges fade into `#07080a` using smooth multi-stop radial gradients (`radial-gradient(circle at 50% 35%, rgba(45, 28, 18, 0.35) 0%, rgba(10, 11, 14, 0.85) 65%, #07080a 100%)`).
2. **Desk & Tray Bed (Tier 1 - Ground Woodwork):** Mahogany and walnut panels are bordered by recessed shadow channels: `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`, `inset 0 -1px 2px rgba(221, 196, 139, 0.12)`.
3. **Velvet Inset Trays (Tier 2 - Recessed Lining):** Deep `#2a0b12` cavities set inside wood cutouts, featuring heavy interior ambient occlusion (`inset 0 6px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`).
4. **Resting Ephemera & Documents (Tier 3 - Floating Parchment):** Parchments, journals, and wax packets hover over the desk with directional candlelight drop shadows: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`, `0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
5. **Hardware & Brass Markers (Tier 4 - Raised Inlays):** Metallic toggles and etched plates employ dual-edge lighting: top edge illuminated by `1px solid rgba(250, 224, 165, 0.45)`, bottom edge anchored by `1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.9)`.
6. **Candle Halo (Ambient Interactive Tier):** Cursor movements softly displace a radial brass-toned warmth across metallic components (`rgba(221, 196, 139, 0.06)`).

## Shapes

The interface embraces tailored, sharp-cornered scholastic craftsmanship (`roundedness: 1` — 0.25rem / 4px base). Documents, wood panels, velvet trays, and brass nameplates avoid circularized modern pill shapes.

- **Document Sheets & Envelopes:** Minimal corner softening (`4px`) with microscopic irregular borders representing natural paper fibers.
- **Drawer Panels & Compartments:** Crisp `2px` to `4px` radii that mimic precise joinery and chisel cuts.
- **Wax Seals & Medallions:** The singular exception to the rectilinear framework; organic, imperfectly rounded silhouettes (`50%` radius with irregular SVG boundary deformities).
- **Brass Placards & Keyholes:** Chamfered or 2px-cornered plates held in place with recessed corner screw heads (`1px` inset details).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Wax Seals
- **Primary Call to Consecration:** A textured velvet burgundy base (`#44121c`) bordered by a `1px` antique brass rim (`#8a7346`). Hover shifts the brass rim to `#fae0a5` with a warm interior glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(221, 196, 139, 0.2)`). Text is rendered in `label-caps` (`Space Mono`).
- **Wax Seal Action:** Circular burgundy medallion (`#6b1d2c`) with an embossed gold monogram insignia in center. Pressing triggers a tactile compression animation accompanied by a gold shimmer pulse.
- **Secondary Brass Key:** Transparent background with an etched outline of `#8a7346`, filling with `#1e140d` on hover.

### Archival Dossier Cards & Drawer Trays
- **Container Structure:** Nested inside a walnut border (`#2d1c12`) with an interior velvet lining (`#2a0b12`).
- **Paper Inserts:** Cards act as stacked aged parchment sheets (`#f7f1e3` text on `#121316` surface, or physical `#ede4d1` parchment backgrounds for letters).
- **Corner Brackets:** Delicate brass filigree L-brackets anchored to the card corners via `1px` CSS borders or SVG vector inlays.

### Form Inputs & Cipher Sliders
- **Text Inputs:** Styled as ruled ledger lines rather than boxed inputs. Border is a single bottom line of `1px solid #8a7346` across `#121316`. Active state intensifies to `#fae0a5` with an ink-feather cursor.
- **Caret:** Soft glowing brass amber bar (`#ddc48b`).
- **Placeholder:** Slanted, low-contrast italic Garamond (`rgba(223, 210, 184, 0.4)`).

### Checkboxes & Binary Selectors
- **Archival Toggle:** Styled as a rectangular brass slide latch or an ink-stamped ledger box (`14px` x `14px`, bordered in `#8a7346`).
- **Checked State:** Marked by a hand-drawn quill stroke or a deep burgundy wax stamp filling the center, flanked by an archival classification tag.

### Metadata Chips & Classification Ribbons
- **Archival Index Chip:** Ultra-compact badges with `#07080a` backgrounds, framed in `1px solid #2b3a4a`, containing cool blue-grey text (`#9eb3c9`) set in `label-xs`.
- **Ceremonial Ribbon Pull:** A vertical silk tab of velvet burgundy extending from behind drawers and letters to signal unread birthday notes or hidden drawer compartments.

### Lists & Ledger Registers
- **Archival Registry:** Dividers use hairline gradients (`transparent` to `#8a7346` to `transparent`) instead of solid border lines.
- **Item States:** Hovering over a list record illuminates the row with an amber candle glow (`rgba(221, 196, 139, 0.04)`) and reveals a hidden monospace timestamp in `archival-steel`.
